## Traffic: source checkpoint still unresolved

The imported `traffic_noexpert_20260831.pth` contains the original pretraining
arguments. The retained fine-tuning recipes are available. What is not recovered
is the original codebook-stage training invocation for its tokenizer.

A CPU-only tensor comparison checked all nine Traffic codebook files currently
under the original repository on port 55040: September 5's best checkpoint,
September 8's best and epoch 3/5/8/10/15 snapshots, and the two copied epoch 3/5
candidates. Each file had 12 comparable encoder tensors, 12 decoder tensors and
6 VQ tensors; zero tensors in each component matched the imported pretraining
checkpoint exactly. Some files are duplicate copies, not independent runs.
These files therefore do not supply an exact tokenizer-weight match. This does
not identify the missing historical hyperparameters, nor prove that a candidate
recipe cannot reproduce the final metric after retraining.
